Behavioral & regulatory specifics

  • Photo, not scan: mid-range Android + WhatsApp-first families photograph documents — the pipeline is already photo/OCR-native.
  • Thermal receipts fade: purchase proof should be captured fast — our retail loop makes photographing receipts natural.
  • Seasonality: school enrollment (June–July), mudik Lebaran (SIM/STNK validity surge), annual pajak — marketing and notification hooks.
  • UU PDP: personal-data law makes "your family's papers never leave your control" a legal-grade trust pitch — our architecture already complies by design.
  • Bahasa-first with local-language content later (story books in Jawa/Sunda/Batak is a differentiator, not a translation chore).

How we differentiate and lead

  1. The loop nobody can copy: documents ⇄ shopping ⇄ roles. Vault competitors can't add nightly retailer intelligence; grocery apps can't hold the family binder. We already operate both halves.
  2. Indonesia-first depth as the moat: STNK pajak, SIM psychotest, KIA schedules, PPDB, arisan, RT/RW iuran — a US/EU competitor would have to rebuild local knowledge per market; we are born in it. Local depth is the global-expansion asset (MY/PH share similar document cultures once ID proves the model).
  3. Free-text model vs category traps: rivals' rigid categories are their convenience and their ceiling; our pattern-suggestions (defaults, never enforced) scale to any document a family has.
  4. Privacy-by-design as marketing: "your family's papers never leave your control" — UU PDP-aligned, no public URLs ever, self-hosted storage layer (DokumenQ). Trust is the vault category's currency.
  5. Plugin platform (#401/#421): documents, groceries, kids, community — each vertical ships as an enablement module; speed of new verticals becomes structural.
